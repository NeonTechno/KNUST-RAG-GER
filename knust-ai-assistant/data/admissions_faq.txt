Admissions FAQ for KNUST E-Learning Centre

1. What are the admission requirements for undergraduate programs?
Applicants must have WASSCE with credits in core subjects (English, Mathematics, Integrated Science or Social Studies) and three elective subjects relevant to the chosen program. Minimum aggregate of 24 for science and 36 for humanities.

2. How do I apply for admission?
Applications are submitted online via the KNUST admissions portal. Create an account, fill the form, upload required documents, and pay the application fee.

3. What is the application fee for Ghanaian and international students?
Ghanaian students pay GHS 200, while international students pay USD 100.

4. Are scholarships available for new students?
Yes, KNUST offers merit-based scholarships, need-based bursaries, and departmental awards. Details are on the financial aid office website.

5. What is the deadline for undergraduate applications?
Typically, the deadline is July 31st for the September intake. Check the admissions website for exact dates.

6. Can I transfer from another university?
Yes, transfer students are considered if they have a minimum GPA of 2.5 and provide transcripts. Credit transfer is subject to departmental approval.

7. What programs are available through the E-Learning Centre?
The E-Learning Centre offers diploma, certificate, and short courses in fields like Education, Business, IT, and Health Sciences.

8. Do I need to take an entrance exam?
Some competitive programs may require an entrance exam or interview. Refer to the specific department for details.

9. How long does the admission process take?
Processing takes 4-6 weeks after submission of a complete application.

10. Where can I get more information?
Visit the admissions website at admissions.knust.edu.gh or contact the admissions office via email or phone.
