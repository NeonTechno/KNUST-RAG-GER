KNUST Campus Navigation Guide

1. College of Engineering: Located near the University Gate, off the Kumasi-Accra Highway. Signs indicate "College of Engineering" towards the northern part of campus.

2. KNUST Library (Main Library): Situated centrally, adjacent to the Great Hall and the Faculty of Law. The library tower is a prominent landmark.

3. Faculty of Business Administration: Found on the eastern side of campus, near the School of Graduate Studies and the International Programmes Office.

4. College of Art and Built Environment: Located northwest of the central campus, near the Department of Architecture and the Housing Directorate.

5. College of Health Sciences: Situated in the southern part of campus, close to the Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital (KATH) and the School of Medical Sciences.

6. University Hospital: Located near the College of Health Sciences, providing medical services to students and staff.

7. Registrar's Department: Found in the Administration Block, west of the Great Hall and near the Vice-Chancellor's office.

8. Finance Office: Situated in the Administration Block, adjacent to the Registrar's Department.

9. Hostels (Halls of Residence): Spread across campus; notable ones include Unity Hall, Independence Hall, Republic Hall, and Queen Elizabeth II Hall. Each hall has distinct signs and entrances.

10. Sports Stadium: Located southeast of the main academic buildings, near the Directorate of Sports Affairs and the Police Station.

11. Directorate of ICT: Found near the College of Engineering, providing internet and technology support.

12. Cashier's Office: Situated in the Administration Block, where students pay fees and collect receipts.

13. International Students' Office: Located near the Faculty of Business Administration, assisting with visas, accommodation, and integration.

14. Chaplaincy: Situated near the Central Cafeteria, offering spiritual services and counseling.

15. Security Headquarters: Located at the main entrance (University Gate), monitoring campus safety and traffic.

16. Agricultural Research Station: Found west of the main campus, near the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) Farm.

17. College of Science: Situated north of the central campus, near the Department of Mathematics and the Physics Building.

18. Faculty of Law: Located east of the Main Library, adjacent to the Faculty of Social Sciences.

19. School of Graduate Studies: Found near the Administration Block, handling postgraduate admissions and affairs.

20. Centre for Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Located near the College of Engineering, supporting startups and student projects.

To navigate, follow the main roads (University Road, Library Road) and look for directional signs. Shuttle services operate between key points during peak hours.
