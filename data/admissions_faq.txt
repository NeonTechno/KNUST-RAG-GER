Admissions FAQ for KNUST E-Learning Centre

What are the admission requirements for undergraduate programs?
Applicants must have a minimum of credit passes (A1-C6) in three core subjects including English Language and Mathematics, and three elective subjects relevant to the chosen program from WASSCE or equivalent.

How do I apply for admission to KNUST?
Applications are submitted online through the KNUST admissions portal at admissions.knust.edu.gh during the application season. Follow the instructions on the portal to create an account, fill the application form, upload required documents, and pay the application fee.

What is the application fee for Ghanaian students?
The application fee for Ghanaian students is GHS 200. For international students, the fee is USD 100.

When is the admission deadline?
The admission deadline varies by program but is typically July 31st for the first semester. Please check the specific program deadline on the admissions portal.

Can I apply for multiple programs?
Yes, you can apply for up to two different programs, but you must pay a separate application fee for each program.

What documents are required for admission?
Required documents include: WASSCE results or equivalent, birth certificate, passport-sized photograph, and for international students, a copy of the passport and proof of English proficiency.

Is there an entrance examination?
Some programs may require an entrance examination or interview. Details will be provided on the admissions portal for each program.

How long does the admission process take?
After submitting a complete application, the review process takes approximately 4-6 weeks. Applicants will be notified via email and the admissions portal.

What is the contact information for the admissions office?
You can contact the admissions office via email at admissions@knust.edu.gh or by phone at +233 3220 63733.

Are there scholarships available?
Yes, KNUST offers various scholarships based on academic merit, financial need, and other criteria. Information about scholarships is available on the financial aid section of the KNUST website.
