KNUST E-Learning Centre Navigation Guide

How to access the E-Learning platform?
Visit the KNUST E-Learning portal at elearn.knust.edu.gh and log in with your student ID and password provided during registration.

What courses are available on the E-Learning platform?
The platform hosts a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses across all colleges, including lecture notes, assignments, and discussion forums.

Is technical support available for the E-Learning platform?
Yes, technical support is available via email at support@elearn.knust.edu.gh or by calling the ICT Help Desk at +233 3220 61111.

How do I submit assignments online?
Assignments can be submitted through the 'Assignments' section of each course on the E-Learning platform. Follow the instructions provided by your lecturer.

Can I access the E-Learning platform on mobile devices?
Yes, the platform is mobile-friendly and can be accessed via any modern web browser on smartphones and tablets.

Are there any fees for using the E-Learning platform?
Access to the E-Learning platform is included in your tuition fees. There are no additional charges for regular use.

What should I do if I forget my password?
Use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page or contact the ICT Help Desk for assistance resetting your credentials.

Is there a tutorial for new users?
Yes, a user guide and video tutorials are available on the dashboard after logging in, and on the KNUST ICT Directorate website.
